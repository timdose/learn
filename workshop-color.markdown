## What you'll learn

- **Munsell Color System**: Understand the basics of the Munsell Color System, including hue, value, and chroma.
- **Color mixing method**: Learn a straightforward method to mix any color.
- **Color theory**: Understand the basics of how light changes color on different surfaces.

## Why take this workshop?

**_Finally_ understand color**: Get a clear, practical way to think about color and color mixing.

**Chat with Tim & other students**: Get access to Tim's private Discord server to ask questions and chat with other students.

**Small groups of 8**: get your questions answered and learn from like-minded artists.
