## Life drawing supplies

#### 14x17” or 18x24” sketch pad

- 50-60 lb paper works well. It will say this on the front of the pad somewhere
- Exact brand is not important. Just decent enough paper, but not so expensive you worry about each sheet you use.
- Suggested: [Strathmore 300 Series Sketch Pad](https://www.dickblick.com/items/strathmore-300-series-sketch-pad-18-x-24-wire-bound-30-sheets/)

#### A board big enough to hold your sketch pad

- Any board that won’t bend will do. Masonite is a common choice
- Suggested: [Blick Sketch Pad Board](https://www.dickblick.com/items/blick-sketch-pad-board-23-12-x-26/)
    - Or just a piece of masonite from the hardware store cut to size!

#### 3-4 #2 or HB pencils

- Later on we can explore more pencils & other media
- Any brand is fine! 
- Suggested: [Ticonderoga Pencils](https://www.amazon.com/Ticonderoga-Pencils-Pre-Sharpened-Yellow-Barrel/dp/B000JEAAP2/) or [Staedtler Lumograph Pencil](https://www.dickblick.com/items/staedtler-lumograph-pencil-hb/)

#### Kneaded eraser

- Any kind is good, as long as it isn’t too mooshy! Should be a little bit firm
- Suggested: [Blick Kneaded Easer](https://www.dickblick.com/items/blick-kneaded-eraser-large/)

## Optional:

#### 1-2 spring clamps

- I always find some kind of use for these—clipping my board to an easel, holding up a shaky easel, etc.
- Suggested: [Mr. Spring Clamps](https://www.amazon.com/Mr-Spring-Clamps-Inches-Heavy/dp/B097PMPT5X/)

#### 2-4 large binder clips

- Another thing I often find a use for! For example clipping paper to my board,
- Suggested: [Spring clamps](https://www.amazon.com/DSTELIN-1-6Inch-Supplies-0-7Inch-Capacity/dp/B07BPJRCSF/)

#### Pen-style eraser

- Useful for cleaning up fine lines. I use these more for portrait drawing or long figure drawings, but sometimes useful in short-pose figure drawing as well
- Suggested: [Tombow Mono-Zero Eraser](https://www.dickblick.com/items/tombow-mono-zero-refillable-eraser-eraser-refill-pack-round/) or [Generals Factis Mechanical Eraser](https://www.dickblick.com/items/generals-factis-mechanical-eraser/)
